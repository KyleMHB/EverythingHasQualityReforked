# Everything Has Quality

## Language

**Quality tier**:
An item's Awful-to-Legendary rating. Quality tier is distinct from how damaged the item is.

**Durability**:
The maximum damage an item or building can withstand, expressed as its maximum hit points. Higher durability means a larger hit-point pool, not automatic repair or a change in quality tier as it wears out.

**Condition**:
An item's remaining hit points, often shown relative to its durability. A damaged item can retain its quality tier.

**Quality assignment**:
Whether this mod adds quality support to an eligible kind of item and manages its quality generation. Quality supplied by vanilla or another mod remains protected.

**Quality effect**:
An optional change to an item's behavior because of its quality tier. Effects are controlled separately from assignment.

**Quality group**:
A broad kind of item with shared default rules, such as resources, buildings or weapons.

**Category branch**:
A named branch of RimWorld's item-category hierarchy, including categories added by mods. A branch rule also covers its descendants unless a more specific rule overrides it.

**Item exception**:
An explicit Include or Exclude rule for one kind of item. Inherit removes that exception and follows the applicable category or group rule.
