# Preserve condition and native dose behavior

Accepted 18 October 2026 in the design interview. A genuine quality change preserves the item's condition percentage; changing settings preserves absolute HP and clamps excess instead of healing. This distinguishes an actual item improvement from repeatedly toggling settings for repairs.

Drug strength and duration are determined from the dose taken while preserving that drug's native redosing/stacking rules. Settings changes do not retroactively amplify an active dose. Unsupported modded drug systems remain unchanged rather than receiving guessed replacements.
